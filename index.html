How The Grading System Works

======================================== THE GRADING ALGORITHM EXPLAINED ======================================== The grading system uses a 100-point scale. It measures the Team's performance using two primary support benchmarks. ---------------------------------------- 1. RESOLUTION RATE (Max 50 Points) ---------------------------------------- GOAL: Close 65% or more of the tickets the team touched during the week. • 65% or higher = 50 points • Below 65% scales down proportionally. ---------------------------------------- 2. BOTTLENECK RATE (Max 50 Points) ---------------------------------------- GOAL: Keep stuck tickets under 5% of total ticket volume. Stalled tickets include "Waiting on Us", "Dev Review", and "In Jira". • 5% or less = 50 points • 15% or higher = 0 points • Between 5% and 15% scales down proportionally. ---------------------------------------- 3. EXTRA CREDIT (LHP Migrations) ---------------------------------------- GOAL: Complete LHP Migrations for bonus. • Each LHP Migration adds +0.2 points to the final score (acts as Extra Credit). ---------------------------------------- FINAL LETTER GRADE SCALE ---------------------------------------- A : 90 - 100 points B : 80 - 89.9 points C : 70 - 79.9 points D : 60 - 69.9 points F : Below 60 points
